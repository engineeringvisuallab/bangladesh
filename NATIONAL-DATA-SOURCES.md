# National data sources

- **OpenStreetMap Bangladesh extract (PBF and `.poly` boundary):** <https://download.geofabrik.de/asia/bangladesh.html>. OSM data is licensed under ODbL; attribution and share-alike requirements apply.
- **Copernicus DEM GLO-90/GLO-30 information and access:** <https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/explore-data/data-collections/copernicus-contributing-missions/collections-description/COP-DEM>. The builder is tested in design for a local GLO-90 GeoTIFF mosaic. Check access rules, source release, CRS, vertical units and license of the specific files you download.
- **pyosmium processing references:** <https://docs.osmcode.org/pyosmium/latest/user_manual/01-First-Steps/> and <https://docs.osmcode.org/pyosmium/latest/user_manual/03-Working-with-Geometries/>.
- **OpenStreetMap copyright and attribution:** <https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright>.

The ZIP intentionally does not bundle these external datasets.
