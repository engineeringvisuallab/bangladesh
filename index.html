A browser-based, game-like 3D world. Terrain and mapped features are rebuilt as 3D objects at geographic coordinates — not displayed as a 2D map. The streaming engine keeps a bounded set of world tiles, looks ahead in the camera direction and switches buildings between detailed footprints within 100 m and merged low-detail blocks farther away.
